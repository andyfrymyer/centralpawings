Coaching staff

Erika Carter

16U head coach

Coach Carter brings more than a decade of experience, including roles at Cedar Cliff and Trinity high schools. She has coached varsity, junior varsity, and AAU basketball and worked in youth development and individual training.

As a player at Trinity, she won the 2001 PIAA Class AA girls basketball state championship. She is an educator in the West Shore School District.

Ways to partner

  • Support the first season

    2027 Founding Partner

    Help cover agreed first-season costs, such as tournament entry fees. Receive featured website recognition, a launch announcement, and a season recap showing what your support funded.

  • Fund practice time

    Court Time Partner

    Cover a set number of practices or gym rentals. We’ll recognize your business on the Wings website and thank you for the sessions you made possible.

  • Help with player fees

    Player Access Partner

    Help cover player fees for families. Receive website recognition and a season-end summary that keeps families’ identities private.

We’ll confirm what your sponsorship funds and how we’ll recognize your business before you commit.

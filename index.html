2027 coaching staff

Erika Carter

16U head coach

Coach Erika Carter brings more than a decade of coaching experience and a strong passion for developing young female athletes to Central PA Wings.

A 2001 PIAA AA Girls Basketball State Champion at Trinity High School, Erika has coached at Cedar Cliff and Trinity High Schools, including varsity assistant and JV head coaching roles. Her experience also includes AAU basketball, youth development programs, and individual player training.

Beyond basketball, Erika is an educator with the West Shore School District and holds a master’s degree in education from Wilson College. She helps young women grow through mentorship and character development on and off the court.

The Wings Way

More than basketball. Built for life.

Central PA Wings exists to develop confident leaders, exceptional teammates, and high-achieving student-athletes through elite basketball, academic excellence, purposeful leadership development, and meaningful service to others.

The Wings Way is our foundation. Five core principles define our culture, guide our actions, and establish the standard for everything we do.

Ways to partner

  • Support the first season

    2027 Founding Partner

    Help cover agreed first-season costs, such as tournament entry fees. Receive featured website recognition, a launch announcement, and a season recap showing what your support funded.

  • Fund practice time

    Court Time Partner

    Cover a set number of practices or gym rentals. We’ll recognize your business on the Wings website and thank you for the sessions you made possible.

  • Help with player fees

    Player Access Partner

    Help cover player fees for families. Receive website recognition and a season-end summary that keeps families’ identities private.

We’ll confirm what your sponsorship funds and how we’ll recognize your business before you commit.
